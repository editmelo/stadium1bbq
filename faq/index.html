Planning your event

What types of events do you cater?

We cater corporate lunches, employee appreciation events, business meetings, community events, birthday celebrations, church events, school functions, family gatherings, and more.

Whether you’re feeding 25 people or planning a larger event, we’ll help you create a menu that fits your occasion.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Indianapolis and surrounding communities, including Speedway, Avon, Plainfield, Brownsburg, Danville, and other areas throughout Hendricks County.

Contact us if your event is outside our normal service area. We may still be able to accommodate you.

Is there a minimum number of guests for catering?

Yes. Our standard catering minimum is 25 guests.

Some special packages and promotional offerings may have different minimums, which will be listed with the package.

Booking and payment

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weekends, holidays, corporate events, and larger gatherings.

Your date is not reserved until the required deposit has been received.

How much is the deposit?

A 30% deposit is required to secure your catering date.

If your event is less than seven days away, full payment is required at the time of booking.

Approved contracted clients may have different payment terms.

What happens if I don’t pay my deposit?

Submitting an inquiry or receiving a quote does not reserve your date.

Until your deposit is received, the date remains available to other customers. If another customer completes their booking and payment first, we may no longer be able to accommodate your event.

Delivery, setup and staffing

Do you offer delivery and setup?

Yes. Delivery and setup are available for catering orders.

Depending on your catering package, we can provide chafing equipment and set up your food so it’s ready for your guests to enjoy.

Delivery and setup fees may vary based on location and event requirements.

Do you provide serving staff?

Serving staff may be available depending on the size and type of event. Let us know what level of service you need when requesting your quote.

The food

Can I customize my menu?

Absolutely.

We offer several catering packages, but we understand every event is different. Tell us about your event, guest count, budget, and menu preferences, and we’ll help you determine the best options.

What are some of your most popular foods?

Customer favorites include our smoked meats, beef bites, pulled pork, pulled chicken, brisket, mac & cheese, mashed potatoes, green beans, rice, cornbread, and signature BBQ bowls.

We also offer seasonal selections throughout the year.

Do you offer vegetarian options?

Yes. Vegetarian options are available, including our mixed vegetable and rice bowl.

Please tell us about dietary needs when requesting your quote so we can discuss available options.

Do you accommodate food allergies?

Please let us know about any food allergies or dietary restrictions before placing your order.

While we will discuss available options with you, our food is prepared in a shared commercial kitchen, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

Do you offer desserts?

Yes. Desserts are available and may include banana pudding, cakes, brownies, cookies, cheesecake cups, dessert shooters, and other seasonal selections.

Ask us what’s available for your event.

Do you offer individual meals or boxed lunches?

Yes. Individual meals, bowl options, and other convenient meal formats may be available for corporate, school, community, and workplace events.

Contact us with your guest count and we’ll help you choose an option.

Do you offer Family Meals?

Yes. Our Family Meals are designed to make feeding your family easier without sacrificing flavor.

Availability, menus, ordering deadlines, and pickup information may vary, so check our Family Meals section for current offerings.

Can I order catering à la carte?

Yes. Select meats, sides, breads, desserts, and other items may be ordered à la carte.

Some specialty items are available by custom quote.

Do you offer seasonal menus?

Yes. We love changing things up throughout the year.

Our seasonal offerings may include chili, white chicken chili, corn chowder, chicken corn chowder, chicken jambalaya, holiday favorites, and other limited-time dishes.

Do you offer tastings?

Tastings may be available for qualifying events. Contact us with your event details to discuss availability and pricing.

Getting a quote

How do I request a catering quote?

You can submit a catering request through our website or contact us directly.

When requesting a quote, please include your event date, estimated guest count, event location, type of event, menu preferences, and any service or setup needs.

The more information you provide, the better we can help you build the right catering experience.

How can I contact Stadium 1 BBQ & More?

Call or text (317) 332-1327 or email stadium1bbq@stadium1bbq.com.

We’re happy to answer questions and help you start planning your event.
