Our story

Cooking with
love

Stadium 1 BBQ & More was built on a passion for great food, genuine hospitality, and cooking with love.

That’s not a slogan somebody wrote for us. It’s the whole business plan.

Shon LaRoi owns and operates Stadium 1 and runs the pit herself. Food made the way it’s supposed to be made — homemade sides, real seasoning, ribs that took the time they needed to take.

What started as feeding people at markets on the weekend turned into catering rooms of 300. The food didn’t change. Just the number of plates.
